# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a respectful, welcoming, and collaborative environment for all contributors, maintainers, and users of Evidence-Grounded Document Intelligence, regardless of experience level, identity, or background.

## Standards & Expectations

- **Constructive Collaboration**: Focus on technical merit, reproducible bug reports, and high-quality code contributions.
- **Respectful Discourse**: Communicate with empathy, professionalism, and openness to feedback.
- **Integrity**: Practice academic and engineering honesty in presenting benchmark figures, citations, and evaluation results.

## Scope & Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for upholding these standards. Unacceptable behavior, harassment, or spam will result in temporary suspension or permanent expulsion from repository discussions.

To report concerns, reach out directly to the maintainers at `rohitam256@gmail.com`.
